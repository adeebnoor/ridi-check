# Code of conduct

This is a short adaptation of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- showing empathy and kindness towards other people;
- respecting opinions, viewpoints and experiences that differ from yours;
- giving feedback constructively and taking it gracefully;
- owning your mistakes, apologising to those affected and learning from them;
- putting what is best for the whole community first.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when
someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub: open the **Security** tab of this repository,
choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say at the top that it is a conduct report. Only the maintainers can
read it. There is no email address for reports. For abuse on GitHub itself, you can also use GitHub's own
**Report content** link next to the comment or profile.

Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainers keep the reporter's
identity private.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do
not follow this code, and will say why when it is appropriate. Depending on the harm done, a response
ranges from a private request to correct the behaviour, through a warning and a temporary ban, to a
permanent ban from the project.
